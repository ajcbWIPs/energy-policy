Diesel Import Reduction Framework

Australia can roughly halve net diesel imports by 2035, from about 29 GL to 15.5 GL a year, and approach zero by about 2045, by electrifying mining, heavy trucks and remote power first, with domestic renewable diesel as a supplement.

Roadmap

  • Emergency, Oct 2026 to Sep 2027. Ring-fence farm and food diesel, revive the fuel task force, demand cuts before rationing, add urea to strategic stocks, fast-track truck conversions and solar pumps, keep canola for domestic crush. Gate: 40+ days of stock, 10 hubs open.
  • Build, 2027 to 2030. About 30 swap and charging hubs beside solar and wind farms on the Hume, Newell, Pacific and Bruce corridors; truck rebates and fuel tax credit conversion; remote towns to 70% renewable; a mining electrification compact; the first renewable diesel plant; halophyte and AgSwap pilots. Gate: imports down 20%.
  • Scale, 2030 to 2035. Half of truck diesel electrified, remote corridors, credits that fund the transition, about 2 GL a year of renewable diesel. Gate: imports down about 47%.
