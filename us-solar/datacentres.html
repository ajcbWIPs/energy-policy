Method and sources

Rooftop potential. NREL TP-6A20-65298 (Gagnon et al. 2016), Table 6. Technical potential for all suitable roofs in the contiguous US at 16% module efficiency, so modern 21% modules lift these figures by roughly 25 to 30%. Large and medium building shares are allocated using the national split (about 35% of generation) because NREL flags state values for those classes as approximate.

Data centre pipeline. DCMap figures as reported by Next Move Strategy Consulting (Aug 2026) for TX, VA, PA, UT, GA and OH, Arizona Corporation Commission for AZ, and company announcements for the Meta Richland Parish, SB Energy Portsmouth and Lea County campuses. Pipeline is development capacity, not operating IT load. Energy demand assumes the load factor slider.

Hubs. Representative coordinates for major operating clusters and announced campuses. Distances are great circle.

Costs. Lazard LCOE+ June 2026 unsubsidised ranges: utility PV $40 to 98, community and C&I PV $88 to 197, CCGT $51 to 129, utility solar plus storage $61 to 156, 4 hour standalone storage $210 to 292 per MWh. Defaults sit near range midpoints and solar LCOE is quoted at a 1,400 kWh/kWp reference site, scaled inversely with the host state's yield. The rooftop adder covers roof lease payments, thousands of separate host contracts and distribution wheeling or virtual PPA settlement. The battery shifting cost is the storage component only, with charging energy counted in solar generation.

Firming logic. Load is flat. Up to 35% of annual load can be met directly while the sun is up. Solar beyond that passes through batteries at 88% round trip efficiency. The remainder of annual load is met by firming generation. Grid supply is a simple tariff and ignores interconnection queue delays, which are now the binding constraint for many campuses.
